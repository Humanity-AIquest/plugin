Look up `$ARGUMENTS` in the Humanities-AI Rights Constitution.

1. Call `hrc_lookup` with the argument. If it is not a clause identifier, treat it
   as a search.
2. Present: the clause title and current text, its rationale, its consensus
   standing, and any related or conflicting clauses.
3. If the clause is contested or currently in voting, say so and offer to show
   the live argument thread with `read_debate`.

Do not paraphrase the clause text. Quote it as it stands, then comment separately.
