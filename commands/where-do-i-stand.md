description: Catch up — what changed in the constitution while you were away, and what needs you
---

Bring the member back up to speed.

1. Call `workspace_resume`.
2. Report, in this order:
   - **Needs you now** — votes closing, replies to their arguments, vouch requests
   - **Changed** — clauses amended or ratified since they were last here
   - **Theirs** — the standing of arguments and amendments they filed
3. Keep it short. Lead with what is time-sensitive; a vote closing in two days
   matters more than a clause that quietly gained consensus.

If nothing needs them, say so in one line. Do not manufacture urgency.
